WHAT THE COMMUNITY BROUGHT

THE STACK CRATES.

Each crate is a contributor's composition of direct, pinned upstream Git submodules. Follow the authors. Clone only the technologies you need. Remix responsibly.

THE GAME

CRATE-DIG. FLIP. REPEAT.

01

Bring the subs.

Bring two or more independent upstream technologies. Share a two-minute demo, give their makers the credit, and submit the GitHub URLs.

02

Pick the stack.

The community selects a composition and two battlers. An accepted contribution is recorded as direct submodules, pinned at specific commits.

03

Pass the mic.

Producers battle and trade creative challenges. The crowd reacts, then sees the media and sources together. The next round builds on the last.
